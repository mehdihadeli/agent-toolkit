# Technical Blog Studio Review

Create or refresh the editorial and technical review checklist for an article workspace.

Examples:

- `/technical-article-assistant:review content/blog/2026-05-22-agentic-memory-boundaries`
- `/technical-article-assistant:review "Productionizing RAG with .NET" --category ai`

Runs: `node "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/technical-article-assistant.js" review $ARGUMENTS`
