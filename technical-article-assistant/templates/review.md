# Review Gate

## Technical Accuracy

- Are all architectural claims defensible?
- Are framework or platform details current?
- Are code snippets aligned with the recommendation?

## Depth

- Does the article move beyond basics?
- Are trade-offs explicit?
- Are failure modes and anti-patterns covered?

## Operational Quality

- Security implications covered
- Observability implications covered
- Deployment and maintenance implications covered
- Cost/performance implications covered

## Publication Readiness

- Thesis is clear in the first section
- Audience is specific
- Conclusion helps readers decide what to do next
- Evidence pack is sufficient
